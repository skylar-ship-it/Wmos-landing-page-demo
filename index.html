Our Approach

Trusted care. Surgical excellence. The standard for full arch.

Full-arch replacement is one of the most transformative procedures in modern dentistry — and one of the most demanding. Here is what we commit to every patient who walks through our doors.

01

Board-Certified Expertise

Every full-arch surgery at WMOS is performed by an oral and maxillofacial surgeon certified by the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. Not by general dentists. Not by traveling specialists. By surgeons who have completed full hospital residency training in oral surgery.

02

Personalized Treatment Planning

Every full-arch case begins with 3D imaging, a thorough medical evaluation, and an honest conversation about your goals, timeline, and budget. We do not believe in one-size-fits-all treatment. Your plan is built around your anatomy, your health, and what you want from this.

03

Comprehensive Anesthesia

Our team includes surgeons certified by the National Dental Board of Anesthesiology. We provide IV sedation in-office, with hospital affiliations available for patients who require it. Your comfort and safety are managed by trained professionals from the moment you arrive.

04

Lifelong Local Care

We are your community oral surgeons — not a chain. Your follow-up care happens in the same office, with the same team, that performed your surgery. Our four offices across West Michigan mean you are never far from the people who know your case best.

The Process

From consultation to permanent restoration.

Full-arch replacement is a journey, not a single appointment. Here is what to expect, step by step, from your first call to your final restoration.

1

Consultation

3D imaging. Medical and dental review. Honest conversation about candidacy and treatment options.

2

Surgical Day

Implant placement under IV sedation. Same-day temporary teeth so you leave with a functional smile.

3

Healing & Follow-Up

Soft food phase, careful monitoring, regular check-ins as your implants integrate with bone.

4

Final Restoration

Permanent zirconia restoration delivered after healing. Built to last. Looks and feels natural.

Frequently Asked Questions

The answers patients ask us most.

Direct, honest answers — the same ones you would get sitting across from us in a consultation.

Single-arch full-mouth reconstruction at WMOS typically ranges from $25,000 to $35,000 depending on bone grafting requirements, anesthesia, and the final restoration material. Full-mouth reconstruction (both arches) ranges from $50,000 to $65,000. We provide transparent pricing during your consultation, with financing available through CareCredit and other partners. We do not believe in surprise billing.

Often, yes. As board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeons with hospital training, we routinely treat patients who were told they had insufficient bone, were too old, or had health conditions that disqualified them from treatment elsewhere. Modern bone grafting, zygomatic implant techniques, and IV sedation protocols allow us to treat patients who could not be treated even ten years ago. The only way to know is a consultation with imaging.

Most patients return to work within three to five days. A soft food diet is required for the first 8 to 12 weeks while the implants integrate with bone. You leave the day of surgery with functional temporary teeth — there is no period of being toothless. The final permanent restoration is typically delivered around month 4 to 6 post-surgery.

Traditional dentures rest on top of the gums and require adhesive. Full-arch implants are anchored permanently to titanium implants placed surgically into the jawbone. They do not slip, click, or come out. They feel and function like natural teeth. Bone density is preserved because implants stimulate the jaw the same way natural tooth roots do. Most patients describe the difference as transformative.

Three reasons. First, every surgery at WMOS is performed by an oral and maxillofacial surgeon — board-certified, hospital-trained, and certified in anesthesia. Not a general dentist who took a weekend course. Second, we are a community practice with deep roots — over 50 years serving West Michigan. Your surgeon will see you for follow-ups, not a rotating roster of out-of-state visitors. Third, we do not pressure-sell. We will tell you honestly whether full arch is the right time and right procedure for you, even if the answer is "not yet" or "consider this less invasive option first."
